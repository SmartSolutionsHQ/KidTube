About this channel

{{brand.tagline}}

A private video channel run by a family on their own computer or server. There are no ads, no recommendations from strangers and no public feed.

Who can watch

Everyone who watches has an account that a parent approved, or a guest code from the family. A guest code works once and gives 24 hours of watching, without hearts or comments.

Be a good guest

Keep comments kind. Ask before saving or sharing someone else’s video. A parent manages accounts, chooses who can post and can remove any comment.

Privacy

The videos stay on the family’s own server. Location details from phones are removed from every upload. This site loads no outside fonts, trackers or analytics.
